# Study plan

Pick the plan that fits your time. Every plan alternates learning, practice aloud and coding. Lessons are long, so on a short plan skim the headings, run the examples and focus on the "Quick check" and the interview tips.

Rule for all plans: after reading, close the book and explain the topic aloud. If you cannot, read it again.

## Daily routine (60 to 90 minutes)

1. 30 minutes: read one lesson section and run its examples.
2. 15 minutes: "Quick check" and "Try it yourself".
3. 20 minutes: interview questions aloud (record yourself once a week).
4. 15 minutes: one coding challenge, or review of missed questions.

## Spaced review

Redo missed questions after 1 day, 3 days and 7 days. Keep a list of red (missed) topics and shrink it every day.

## The day before the interview

- Read the [quick answers cheatsheet](cheatsheets/interview-quick-answers.md) and the [event loop cheatsheet](cheatsheets/event-loop-and-async.md).
- Do one short mock. Prepare your questions to ask. Sleep well.
